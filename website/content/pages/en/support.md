## Contact the Serlink team

Email [support@serlink.alkinum.com](mailto:support@serlink.alkinum.com?subject=Serlink%20Support) for product help, private security reports or privacy requests.

For a reproducible public bug or feature request, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/backrunner/Serlink/issues). Issues are public; use email for private reports.

## Include the useful details

- Serlink version and operating system version.
- The action that failed and the approximate time.
- Whether this is an App Store or direct build.
- A screenshot or redacted diagnostic export, if it helps and you choose to share it.

Diagnostic exports are available in **Settings → Diagnostic logs**. Review them before sending. Do not include passwords, private keys, recovery keys, MCP tokens or private terminal output.

## Try a guide first

[Connection troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting), [vault recovery](/docs/vault) and [sync guidance](/docs/sync) cover common issues. Check [release availability](/download) if you cannot find an installer.

Email opens your own mail application. This website does not upload diagnostics or submit a support request for you. See [privacy](/privacy) for handling of messages you choose to send.
